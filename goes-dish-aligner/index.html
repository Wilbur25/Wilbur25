Waiting for spectrum data from the Pi.

Rough guide for HRIT: a steady lock with SNR above about 4 dB decodes; 6 dB or more gives margin for rain. When peaking, move in small steps and wait two or three seconds each time, because the readings average over a moment.

    Sun outages

    Twice a year the sun passes behind the satellite and its noise swamps the signal for a few minutes a day. With a wide L-band beam you'll see SNR dips on the days around the peak.
